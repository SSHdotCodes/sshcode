# Bots

A bot is a personal agent you talk to in plain English. It runs your chats for you: it can start, check, steer and tidy them, tell you what happened, and your other chats can message it. Bots are off by default and use no tokens while off.

## Turn bots on

Open **Settings > Bots** on web or desktop, or **Settings › Bots** on iPhone, and turn on **Bots**. Then choose **New bot**: pick a color and a name, the model it runs on (any model in the picker), an optional personality, and what it may do. Creating a bot opens its chat.

Bots appear at the top of the sidebar on the Mac and in a strip at the top of the chat list on iPhone. Their own chats don't appear in your chat list or search.

## What a bot may do

- **Manage chats**: start, message, rename and tidy chats for you. Off, it only reads and reports.
- **Answer questions**: answer other chats' questions without asking you. Off, it asks you first.
- **Use subagents**: hand work to other models when it helps.

A bot treats what other chats, web pages and files say as information, not as your instructions. Before anything hard to undo, such as stopping or settling chats, it asks you first unless you asked for exactly that.

## Watching chats and messages from other chats

Ask a bot to keep an eye on chats ("tell me when the phone build finishes"). Updates arrive together as one note, at most every couple of minutes; failures and questions arrive at once. Any chat's agent can also message your bot, and you can send a chat to it from the chat's menu with **Send to <bot>…**. The bot only writes back when you'd want to know now.

## Memory and routines

A bot keeps short dated notes about your preferences. Open the bot's info panel (the info button in its chat header, or its header on iPhone) to read, edit or delete them, see the chats it watches, and turn its routines on or off. Routines are schedules you ask for, such as "every morning at 9, tell me what happened overnight"; a bot has no background check-ins of its own.

**Pause** stops a bot from waking for updates, messages and routines; they wait, and you'll see how many. Resume to get them as one note. **Delete** removes the bot, its chat, its notes and its routines; your other chats stay.
