# Agent settings

Open **Settings > Agents** on web or desktop. On iPhone, open **Settings** and tap **Agents** under **Agents & models**; the row lists any levels you changed from Default.

Five sliders shape how agents work in every chat: **Token use**, **Subagents**, **Questions**, **Checking work** and **Replies**. **Default** on each slider is SSHCode as it works today, and a small dot above its stop marks it. Changes apply to new turns. **Reset all to Default** puts every slider back.

## Where changes are saved

On web and desktop, the sliders change the environment chosen at the top of the page, usually this Mac. On iPhone, a change goes to every connected machine, and the screen shows the most recent choice among them, whichever device made it.

## Subagents at None

**None** turns subagents and councils off in every chat. Each chat's own **Subagents** switch shows off with "Off in Settings › Agents" and cannot be turned on. Your per-chat choices are kept and come back when you move the slider off None; a chat whose own switch is off stays off at every level.

## Token cost

Once measured, **Token use** shows how many more tokens and how much more plan usage a level takes than the harness's own app or CLI. Hover the harness name on web, or tap it on iPhone, to compare harnesses.
